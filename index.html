Company overview

Practical transport solutions. Professional partnerships.

Kholie's Fleet (Pty) Ltd is a South African private company established on 1 September 2023 and operating from Soweto, Gauteng. The company is positioned to support clients requiring logistics coordination, transportation services and fleet-related support.

Our approach centres on understanding client requirements, coordinating suitable transport solutions and maintaining professional communication throughout each assignment. We aim to facilitate the safe and timely movement of goods, equipment and property-related items through accountability and operational reliability.

We welcome opportunities with government departments, municipalities, corporate procurement teams, property companies, logistics operators and other organisations requiring suitable transport and fleet-related services.
